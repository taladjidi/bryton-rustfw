# Review 2026-10-31: the whole repository, the roadmap and the UI on the unit

What this file holds: the findings of the 2026-10-31 audit of the whole repository (performance, safety, architecture,
testability, the UI on the unit against the simulator and against Garmin and Wahoo), each with its fix and its state,
so the fixes can be tracked to their merge; and the roadmap's progress with the next features. What does not belong:
the dated findings of a ride (journal.md), what is built (features.md), what the unit has not shown yet (status.md).

Method, on main at b19f8aa (441 commits and 38 k lines since the last review, docs/review-2026-10-29.md):
- the gate `./check.sh` (green: 1 347 host tests, 198 doc tests, image 799 020 B, every stack frame in budget);
- the unit, cable in, from a RAM run of that build: `tools/walk.py --rides` (132 paths: 45 walked, 84 refused by its
  own safety rules, 66 of them imperial, 3 aborted after a Start that did not open the ride menu), `tools/profile.py`
  (idle, the ride pages, the menu, a ride), `tools/dev.py info` (fault marker clear, one radio reset in the session);
- `cargo mutants` on src/storage/recorder/mod.rs, rec.rs and fat/write.rs (an hour's cap), jscpd over src and tools,
  `cargo llvm-cov` on the host tests (installed for this review), and the new `tools/panics.py` (every panic site of
  the release ELF, merged in 9747ab6);
- six read-only review passes: the recording path, the state files and the boot (Opus, the diff since c0d8368);
  performance (Sonnet, then Opus on the parts the first pass did not reach); architecture and tests (Sonnet); the
  roadmap against the code (Sonnet); the unit's shots against the simulator's (Opus); the UI against Garmin's and
  Wahoo's documented screens (Opus, sources cited at the end).

State: `[ ]` open, `[x]` merged on main, `[-]` not done on purpose (the reason follows). Severity: H (a ride, data
or the gate at stake), M (wrong, a measured cost, or a rule broken), L (small).

**Verdict:** nothing found that loses a ride; the recording path holds its 2026-10-29 shape. Fix first: the three
new files that write the recorder's directory sector (1), the three stores at their limit that fail silently (2), and
the recorder rules no test guards (3). Then stop adding features until the pass's cost is back where it was (6 to 10)
and the unit has ridden what the simulator holds (roadmap, block 4).

## Must fix

1. `[ ]` **H. bikes.bin, places.bin and `\Rustfw\Log` are created in the `\Rustfw` directory sector that S7 closed**
   (storage/recorder/mod.rs:451 `KEPT` has six entries; app/bikes.rs:259 and app/places.rs:123 through
   editor.rs:762 `write_copy_in`; storage/faultlog.rs:189 `ensure_dir`).
   - What this is: S7 (2026-10-29) made the recorder create every app file at a ride's start, so no later write
     touches the directory sector that names `ride.rec`, `Rides`, `Activities` and `Discarded`.
   - Problem: three files added since are created on their first write, which rewrites that sector; bikes.bin is
     written on every unit after the update with no rider action (the migration sets `dirty`). The writes wait for
     `ride_settled()`, so the ride in progress is safe; the kept rides and the full copies are what a torn sector
     costs.
   - Fix: `(BIKES_FILE, 2 * SECTOR_U32)` and `(PLACES_FILE, 2 * SECTOR_U32)` in `KEPT`, the `Log` folder made in
     `make_kept` (and in its `whole` check), the S7 cut test extended to them. Agent: critical.
   - If skipped: a power cut during that one write can lose the names of the kept rides.
2. `[ ]` **H. Three stores sit at their limit and two fail silently** (src/lib.rs:794 `App` is 65 520 B of a
   65 536 B budget; app/settings_file.rs:109 `data` is 192 B with 173 used and `put` drops what does not fit, so the
   length field lies; ui/page.rs: pages.bin ends at byte 511 of 512).
   - What this is: the app's state cell in AXI SRAM, the settings file's data block, the pages file.
   - Problem: the next field breaks the gate (fine, it is loud); the next setting is written as nothing, read back as
     its default, with no test failing; the next page byte has nowhere to go.
   - Fix: `put` asserts on overflow (one line, one test); print `App`'s field sizes and move the two or three largest
     parts into their own `make_*` cells (main.rs); the sector has room for 480 B of settings data (`CRC_AT` minus
     `DATA_AT`), so size `data` from those constants; plan pages.bin v2. Agent: implementer (sonnet).
   - If skipped: the next agent's setting silently does not persist.
3. `[ ]` **H. The recorder's rules that no test guards** (cargo-mutants, 44 of the 209 mutants tested in the hour survived,
   all in recorder/mod.rs, 1 160 planned over the three files: keep_up:569 the two copies' checkpoint coupling and its `beats_only`
   exception, every operator; give_up_save:715 the `Saving` guard; rescue:750; the `Ram` sink's bounds at 476;
   io_after:581 to 599; resumed_after:611; set_profile:631; put:664; rescued:814; starting:850; second:880 and 883; the `RECORD_MAX` arithmetic at 322).
   - What this is: the checkpoint cadence between the clean and the full copy, the give-up path, the rescue, the
     in-RAM sink.
   - Problem: AGENTS.md says a ride build is tested first; a change to any of these lines is noticed by no test.
   - Fix: one test per rule: the full copy wanting a checkpoint makes the clean one checkpoint in the same pass; a
     paused full copy with a minute of beats alone does not; a give-up outside `Saving` does nothing; a write past the
     RAM tail is refused; a record of `RECORD_MAX` bytes fits with `LOW` to spare. Then `tools/review.py` on the
     branch to confirm. Agent: critical for the tests on the recorder.
   - If skipped: the next recorder change can break the checkpoint coupling and pass the gate.

## Should fix

4. `[ ]` **M. The fault log loses its line when the unit resets before the resumed ride is saved** (src/lib.rs:709
   clears `FAULT_BKP` after 60 s; app/faultlog.rs keeps the line in RAM until `ride_settled()`).
   - Problem: a panic mid-ride, the ride resumes, then a power-off or a flat battery before the save: the fault is
     never written to `faults.log`, and the marker is gone.
   - Fix: at the minute, clear only the count byte while the line is not logged; clear the word once it is.
   - If skipped: the faults that matter most (mid-ride) are the ones never seen.
5. `[ ]` **M. A cable pull right after the end card resets the unit mid-save** (app/screens.rs:128 `busy()` lacks
   `!ride_settled()`; main.rs gives `busy()` to `policy.cable`).
   - Problem: the save resumes at boot, but the trip totals (`SettingsStore::saving`), the segment efforts not yet
     written and a pending fault line are RAM only and lost.
   - Fix: `busy()` also true while `!ride_settled()`.
6. `[ ]` **M. The pass costs half as much again as two days ago, and nothing measured it** (tools/profile.py from
   RAM, cable in, the same method as 2026-10-29: the overview idle 1.52 % awake against 0.90 to 1.10 %; a pass 657 to
   677 us; 3 of the 441 commits since carry an awake figure, 78 an image size).
   - Problem: AGENTS.md asks for the time awake in the commit message of anything drawn each second; the agents wrote
     the image size and not the cost, so the regression went through 441 merges.
   - Fix: `tools/walk.py` already ends with a 20 s profiler window on the overview: print its awake share and fail
     over a bound written in docs/power.md (a `numbers.py` marker set at each merge the owner accepts), and make the
     runner's merge step run `tools/profile.py idle` when the unit is connected.
   - If skipped: the battery pays for every feature, unseen.
7. `[ ]` **M. The status strip is the largest section of every pass** (src/lib.rs:712 to 715: `gps.note_fix`,
   `rec.mark`, `update_strip` each pass; measured 143 us mean, 2.8 ms max, 0.30 % of wall on every screen, while the
   panel gets 727 px/s).
   - Problem: the strip's inputs change once a minute (the clock) or on an event (battery, GPS, the ride, USB), yet
     the parts are rebuilt and compared 21 times a second, and something in it costs 2.8 ms about once a second.
   - Fix: split the section (the `ahead()` lookup, the compare, the draw) with two more `Sec` marks to see which
     part it is, then update the strip only when an input changed. Measure with `tools/profile.py idle`.
8. `[ ]` **M. The radio receive spins 200 us after every burst** (src/main.rs:917 to 930 `nrf_recv`: after the
   last byte it polls the UART for `CPU_HZ / 5000` cycles with no WFI; app/radio.rs:116 calls it each pass).
   - Problem: with three or four ANT+ sensors at about 4 Hz, 12 to 16 of the 20 passes a second get a frame, 2.4 to
     3.2 ms/s, an estimated 0.24 to 0.32 % awake on a ride (the `radio` section is 40 us mean at 42 calls/s idle).
   - Fix: wait two byte times (20 us at 1 Mbaud); the long wait only after 16 bytes with no gap, when RTS held the
     chip. Check the frame drop count on the unit before and after (BOARD.md says what RTS does).
9. `[ ]` **M. A running ride keeps 20 passes a second for the whole ride** (app/device.rs:209 `pass_ms`:
   `POLL_MS` 50 while `busy()`, 250 idle; the pass's fixed cost is the whole awake figure: second 70 us, strip 143,
   gnss 89, radio 80, draw 60 to 83, files 58, altitude 41, usb 37, trainer 32, scan 25, recorder 21).
   - Fix: 100 ms passes while a ride runs with no finger, tone or banner (keys wake the loop by interrupt already;
     the Nordic chip's FIFO and RTS hold the bytes; check the touch sampling). Estimated gain: about 0.3 % awake, a
     third of a ride's idle cost.

## The roadmap: progress and what comes next

Progress validated: the 12 struck steps spot-checked across blocks 1 to 3 (T5, S5, auto lap, the lap list, QUICK
STATUS, the ready check, zones, BATT HRS, BATT SAVER, three bikes, descents, the power curve) exist as described, each
with a sim test; the gaps are in depth (T5's lux edges only unit-tested; no test that a discarded ride writes no
effort; auto lap by time untested; descents on the road ahead without a course untested; the curve test checks
colours, not values). Blocks 1, 2 and 3a to 3c are done in the simulator; the checklist counts 40 features seen on
the unit against 107 simulator only, status.md 43 "sim only" bullets, all from the last two days.

Block 4, the unit gate, is the roadmap's own next step and the audit's: nothing of the last 441 commits has been
ridden. Grouped by session (the dev link alone needs no one; a card write, a flash or a ride needs the owner):
- A, desk, dev link only: STORAGE and ABOUT, FONT = BARLOW, the themes' layouts, page switches and names, QUICK
  STATUS, the gauge fields' time awake, the history screens (curve, load, MONTHS, laps on a stock ride), the ready
  check, the units and clock fields, the stack canary on a cold boot, the loop policy's key wake, the Nordic watchdog;
- B, desk, owner present: an `EMMC_WRITE=1` build (three bikes, the segment efforts, screenshots, the fault log,
  DELETE, pages.bin, the scan cache, SAVE PLACE, GIVE UP SAVE, the late-card boot), then the computer and the phone
  (USB suspend and drops, the iPhone link, Di2, the power meter's calibration, the strap's battery, AssistNow,
  `tools/sync.py` against the real services);
- C, the trainer (D900): RIDE A COURSE, level mode, release, power match, the ramp test, spin-down, FTMS and FE-C,
  an indoor ride saved;
- D, an outdoor ride with the strap and the meter: heart rate and HRV in the full copy, TE / PS, auto lap, the
  alerts, the sunset alert, BATT HRS, BATT SAVER, GPS POWER, the profiles with a mid-ride restart, THEME AUTO at
  dusk, OUTDOOR and LIGHT in the sun, the barometric altitude, the map's time awake;
- E, a real climb or a followed course: CLIMBS DONE, climbs ahead and the pacer, peaks and cols, reverse and BACK TO
  START, STEEP DESCENT, the live segments.

Next, in this order: the Must fix lines above (1 to 3); the pass's cost back to 1 % (6 to 10, measured); session A
by the dev link; the UI batch (16), since the shots decide it without a ride; then with the owner B, C, D, E; stop
mode between seconds (the largest battery gain left, critical, owner present); `tools/sync.py` and
`tools/segments.py own` on real rides against Intervals.icu; the MTB leftovers (touch for gloves, the track's
resolution) and IGN packs per region. The phone app (block 5) stays behind the gate.

Strike or park: languages beyond English (fonts beyond ASCII, an image cost, for an English reader); key click (the
keys have no beep to switch); ANT+ radar, lights, e-bikes and the sensor pool (no such equipment to test); routing on
the unit (large, and courses over USB cover it); updating the firmware from a file on the volume (AGENTS.md forbids
touching IAP.bin or the bootloader, and Engineer Mode is the way back).
